How to use keyword research

Start with the topic your audience is already searching for. Expand it into specific phrases that match a clear question, problem, comparison, or task. Then choose topics that you can answer better than existing results.

Long-tail keyword examples

Instead of targeting only a broad phrase such as “seo tools”, explore specific variations such as “free seo tools for beginners”, “seo audit tool for small websites”, or “how to use a keyword research tool”. Specific phrases often make it easier to build focused pages.

Search intent matters

Classify ideas as informational, commercial, or transactional based on what the searcher appears to want. Create content that satisfies that intent instead of forcing a keyword into an unrelated page.

Build a topic cluster

Use one main topic page and supporting articles that answer narrower questions. Link related pages together with descriptive internal links so readers and search engines can understand the relationship.
