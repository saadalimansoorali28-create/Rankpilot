What a sitemap does

An XML sitemap gives search engines a machine-readable list of URLs you want them to discover. It does not guarantee indexing or rankings.

Which URLs should you include?

Use canonical, indexable URLs that you want search engines to discover. Avoid including duplicate, redirected, or blocked URLs unless you have a specific technical reason.

Submit your sitemap

After publishing your sitemap at a stable URL such as /sitemap.xml, you can submit it through your search engine webmaster tools.
