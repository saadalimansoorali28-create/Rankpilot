Start with the viewer's search

Choose a topic that clearly describes the problem or goal your video solves. Add specific modifiers such as tutorial, beginner, comparison, review, or step by step when they accurately describe the video.

Write a useful title

Keep the main topic clear and make the benefit understandable. Avoid repeating the same phrase unnaturally or promising something the video does not deliver.

Use descriptions naturally

Explain what viewers will learn near the beginning of the description. Include related phrases where they fit naturally, then add useful links or resources.
