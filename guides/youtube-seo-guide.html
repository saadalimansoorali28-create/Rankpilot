YouTube SEO Guide: From Keyword to Video

YouTube SEO starts with understanding the viewer's problem and making a video that actually solves it. Metadata supports discovery, but the content itself must deliver on the promise.

Choose a focused topic

Start with a specific question, task, or outcome. Avoid combining unrelated topics into one video just to capture more keywords.

Build a clear title

Make the main subject obvious and communicate the value of the video. Avoid misleading claims and unnecessary repetition.

Write the opening description

Explain what the viewer will learn in the first part of the description. Add related terminology naturally and provide useful links when appropriate.

Use related phrases

Related phrases can help you understand the topic's language and create a better outline. Do not treat tags or repeated phrases as a substitute for useful content.

Review after publishing

Use your actual audience data and performance reports to improve future topics, titles, and content decisions.
