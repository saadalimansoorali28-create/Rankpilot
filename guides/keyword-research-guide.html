How to Do Keyword Research for SEO

Keyword research is the process of discovering the language people use when looking for information, products, services, or solutions. Good research connects those searches to pages that genuinely satisfy them.

1. Start with a seed topic

Choose a broad phrase that describes your subject. Then expand it with modifiers such as beginner, guide, tool, examples, comparison, price, or how to when those modifiers fit the topic.

2. Prefer specific opportunities

Broad keywords can describe a huge range of needs. Long-tail phrases usually give you a clearer idea of what a searcher wants, making it easier to create a focused page.

3. Group similar keywords

Do not create a separate page for every tiny variation. Group phrases that can be answered by the same useful resource and use headings to cover meaningful subtopics.

4. Map intent to content

Informational searches often need guides or explanations. Commercial searches may need comparisons or category pages. Transactional searches often need a direct path to an action.

5. Turn research into a content plan

Choose a primary topic, supporting questions, a clear audience, and an internal-link path. Then publish the page that best satisfies the topic rather than trying to repeat the exact keyword everywhere.
