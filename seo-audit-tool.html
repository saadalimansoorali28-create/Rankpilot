Check important on-page SEO signals and identify simple improvements for your page HTML.

Open SEO Audit

What the audit checks

The current browser-based audit checks the page title, meta description, canonical tag, H1 count, mobile viewport tag, and missing image alt text when you provide page HTML.

Why on-page basics matter

Clear titles and descriptions help communicate page topics. A sensible heading structure improves document organization, while descriptive image alternative text can make images more accessible.

What this tool does not do

This lightweight version does not replace a full crawler, backlink analysis, log analysis, or live ranking data. Use it as a quick checklist before deeper SEO work.
